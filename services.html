FAQ

Before you reach out.
Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes — most communication happens over email and calls, so timezone is rarely a blocker. Payments can be arranged via bank transfer or common online payment methods.

Can you take over an existing Laravel project?

Yes. I'll review the codebase first, flag any structural issues, and give you an honest estimate before committing to changes or new features.

Do you build the backend and the app together?

That's the most efficient setup — a Laravel REST API paired with a Flutter app means one shared source of truth for your data, instead of two disconnected systems.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief of the problem you're solving, any existing brand assets, and access to relevant accounts (hosting, Firebase, etc.) once we agree on scope.

Do you offer support after launch?

Every package includes a support window for fixes. Ongoing monthly retainers are available for ongoing updates, monitoring and new features.
