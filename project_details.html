Kite — Banking for freelancers

Industry Fintech
Client Kite Financial Inc.
Completed On 2025
Live Preview kite.app ↗

Kite needed a banking app that felt honest about how freelance income actually works.

Irregular, tax-heavy, and hard to plan around — the existing dashboard treated freelance income like a salaried paycheck. I led the product design from research through launch, rebuilding the account dashboard around cash-flow forecasting instead of a static balance, and reworking onboarding so new users could see their real tax liability within their first session.
