How I work

A few things I hold onto on every project.
01

Start from the problem

Every engagement starts with the constraint, not the mood board — what's actually broken for the user, and why it hasn't been fixed yet.

02

Design in the medium

I prototype in code early, not just static screens, so what ships matches what was approved — no surprises at handoff.

03

Fewer, sharper decisions

One strong idea, executed with restraint, beats five decent ones competing for attention on the same screen.
