Flash Innovation

Role Product & Web Designer
Year 2026
Category Web Design

Flash Innovation approached me to rebuild their marketing site ahead of a product launch, moving away from a static one-pager toward a system that could scale as new features shipped.

I designed a modular content architecture — reusable section templates, a flexible image gallery, and a component-driven build — so the team could publish updates without waiting on a developer each time.

The new site cut page-load time significantly and gave the team a consistent visual language across every launch asset.
